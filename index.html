🔍 What This Application Does

kws-x1-standalone-rs replaces the factory firmware inside two popular USB-C power meters: the KWS-X1 and the UT01C. Once installed, the meter no longer behaves like a simple display. It becomes a small measuring tool that shows you exactly how much power a phone, laptop, charger, power bank or cable is carrying, and it can also ask a charger for a specific voltage so you can test devices on your own terms.

You do not need a soldering iron, a programmer or any developer tools to use the finished firmware. You only need the download, your meter, and a normal Windows computer with a USB port. The firmware is written in Rust and runs directly on the meter's own chip, which is why it is fast, stable and fully open source.

✨ Key Features

  • Live power readings - voltage, current and wattage update continuously on screen.
  • Ah and Wh counters - track how much energy and charge has passed through the meter during a test.
  • Graphs and ripple display - see voltage over time and spot unstable chargers at a glance.
  • USB PD, including EPR - negotiate Power Delivery contracts, extended power range included.
  • Fixed, PPS and AVS triggers - request a set voltage, or fine-tune with PPS and AVS up to 28 V and 30 V.
  • Quick Charge 2.0 and 3.0 - trigger classic QC modes on older chargers.
  • AFC, SCP and FCP - support for additional fast-charge protocols used by many phone brands.
  • Cable e-marker reading - check the rating and capabilities stored inside a USB-C cable.
  • 5 A current test - confirm whether a cable and charger really deliver high current.
  • PD sniffing - watch the messages a charger and a device exchange during negotiation.
  • Calibration - correct small measurement differences and keep readings accurate.

📊 Reading the Live Screen

The main screen is designed to be read at a glance. The large numbers show voltage and current right now, while the wattage number tells you the real power flowing at this moment. Below those numbers you will find the running Ah and Wh totals, which keep adding up until you reset them, so you can measure a full charging session from empty to full.

The graph view draws the last few seconds or minutes of voltage and current so you can see dips, spikes and wobbles that plain numbers would hide. The ripple view zooms in on small voltage fluctuations, which is a quick way to compare one charger against another. A steady line means a calm, well-behaved supply. A jagged line tells you the charger is struggling under load.

⚙️ Asking a Charger for a Specific Voltage

The trigger menu lets you request a fixed voltage, or use PPS and AVS to sweep smoothly through a range. This is useful when you want to check how a device behaves at 5 V, 9 V, 15 V or 20 V, or when you want to confirm that a charger really supports extended power range. Quick Charge, AFC, SCP and FCP options appear in the same menu for chargers that use those protocols instead of USB PD.

Always start with the lowest voltage and raise it in small steps. Never request more voltage than the device you are testing can accept, and never trigger a voltage with nothing connected. If the display goes blank or the charger clicks off, unplug everything, wait a moment, and start again at the lowest setting. The 5 A test and the cable e-marker reader work the same way: connect, read, then disconnect before changing settings.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

  • The meter is not recognised. Try a different USB-C cable and a different USB port. Some cables carry power only and cannot pass data.
  • The application opens but stays empty. Close it, unplug the meter, plug it back in, then reopen the application.
  • The screen looks frozen. Disconnect the load, let the meter rest for a few seconds, then reconnect.
  • Numbers look slightly off. Use the calibration option to correct the readings against a known good reference.
  • A trigger is refused. The charger does not support that voltage or protocol. Try a lower voltage or a different mode.
  • Windows shows a security prompt. Confirm that you are running the package from the official project page, then continue.
